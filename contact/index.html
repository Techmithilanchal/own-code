Frequent Questions

How do you keep costs low? +
We use a highly streamlined, remote development center which cuts down geographic workspace premiums. Furthermore, we optimize compute budgets by fine-tuning smaller, task-specific open-source models rather than routing all queries through expensive proprietary general APIs.
How is my company IP protected? +
We sign comprehensive SLAs and NDA terms before any data exchange. We do not use your private database items or customer chat logs to train public foundation models. We also offer deployment directly inside your own private clouds (VPC).
What is a typical deployment timeline? +
A custom document-based RAG Search or conversational Chatbot typically takes 2 to 4 weeks from planning to production. Complex multi-step Autonomous Agents requiring multi-software integrations can take between 4 to 8 weeks.
Can we deploy models on our servers? +
Yes! We specialize in deploying local vector databases and open-source models (like Llama-3 or Mistral) on your dedicated AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure VMs. This gives you full compute independence with zero recurring API costs.
