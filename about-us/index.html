Our Operating Philosophy

Absolute IP Protection

We never use client-proprietary data to train public models. When we build your chatbot or RAG search, the code, models, and embeddings belong 100% to you and are deployed securely inside your cloud environment.

Compute Cost Optimization

We don't lazily plug expensive GPT-4 endpoints when Llama-3-8B fine-tuned for $50/month can do the same job. We optimize embedding architectures and host on serverless GPUs, cutting your monthly software costs.

Zero-Hallucination RAG

We build systems that cite the page, document, and paragraph of their sources. If the information isn't in your files, the AI is programmed to state that clearly rather than inventing facts.
